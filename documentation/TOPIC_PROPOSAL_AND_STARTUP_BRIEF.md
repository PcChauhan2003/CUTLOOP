## 1. Project Title & Vision
* **Project Name:** **CUTLOOP** — AI-Powered Industrial Waste-to-Value Marketplace
* **Tagline:** *"One factory's leftover material is another creator's raw material."*
* **Core Problem Domain:** Circular Economy, Industrial Textile Sustainability & B2B E-Commerce.
* **Target Users:** 
  1. **Sellers:** Textile composite mills, garment export houses, and fabric processing factories (Ahmedabad, Surat, Tirupur, Ludhiana) burdened with uncataloged pre-consumer cutting offcuts, roll remnants, and deadstock.
  2. **Buyers:** Sustainable boutique apparel brands, zero-waste fashion designers, craft studios, and circular upcycling makers seeking high-grade fabric remnants at 40%–60% below virgin fabric spot rates.

## 4. Key AI Capabilities Planned & Built
1. **Automated Weave Identification (MobileNetV2):** Upload a single smartphone photo $\rightarrow$ Classify Cotton, Denim, Silk, Polyester, Wool, Linen with $>94\%$ test accuracy.
2. **Defect Inspection (YOLO):** Detect surface tears, stains, holes, and weave fissures with spatial bounding boxes.
3. **Transparent Explainability (Grad-CAM):** Visual attention heatmaps displaying exact warp-and-weft feature regions.
4. **Predictive Resale Price Valuation:** Suggests fair market resale pricing (₹/kg) based on fabric type, weight, and condition grade.
5. **Seasonal Demand Forecasting:** Time-series alerts forecasting next-month demand shifts across regional textile clusters.
